1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy describes how TripEase (“TripEase”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use the TripEase mobile application for iOS, Android, and iPad, and when you visit this website. For privacy questions, contact us at apptripease@gmail.com.

2. Summary

  • TripEase does not require you to create an account.
  • We do not use a third-party analytics SDK, do not show ads in the app, and do not track your device location.
  • Travel content (countries, airports, forms, advisories) is loaded from Firebase / Firestore operated by Google.
  • Optional TripEase Pro subscriptions are processed by Apple or Google via RevenueCat. We receive an anonymous purchase identifier and purchase status — not your full payment card details.
  • Map tiles may be requested from OpenStreetMap (which can see your IP address).
  • If you open a partner recommendation, you leave TripEase and that partner’s own privacy policy applies.

3. Information we collect

3.1 Information you provide

If you email us (for support, partnerships, or privacy requests), we receive the content of your message and your email address so we can reply. We do not operate in-app user accounts or profiles.

3.2 App content and configuration

The app downloads travel reference data (for example country notes, airport guides, arrival-form links, and partner listings) from Firebase / Cloud Firestore. That content is the same travel information published for users; it is not a personal travel journal tied to your identity.

Preferences you set on your device (such as selected destinations or checklist progress) stay on your device unless a future feature explicitly syncs them — in which case we will update this policy before that happens.

3.3 Purchases (TripEase Pro)

If you subscribe to TripEase Pro, Apple App Store or Google Play processes the payment. We use RevenueCat to manage entitlements. In connection with that, we may process:

  • An anonymous app user / purchase identifier
  • Subscription product, status, renewal, and expiration information
  • Platform (Apple or Google) and related purchase metadata needed to unlock Pro features

We do not receive your full credit or debit card number. Payment details are handled by Apple or Google under their terms and privacy policies.

3.4 Technical data from network requests

When the app loads content or map tiles, standard network information such as IP address and device/user-agent details may be processed by the providers listed in section 5 (for example Google Firebase, OpenStreetMap). We do not use that traffic to build advertising profiles.

3.5 What we do not collect

  • No account registration or social login
  • No advertising identifiers for ads (the app has no ads)
  • No continuous location tracking or background GPS logging
  • No third-party analytics SDK for behavioral tracking

4. How we use information

We use the information described above to:

  • Deliver and update travel content in the app
  • Provide optional TripEase Pro features and restore purchases
  • Respond to support and privacy emails
  • Maintain security, fix bugs, and improve reliability
  • Comply with legal obligations where applicable

5. Sharing and third parties

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with service providers needed to run TripEase, or when you choose to open an external link.

5.1 Firebase / Firestore (Google)

Used to host and deliver app content (countries, airports, forms, advisories, and related listings). Google may process technical data such as IP address when the app fetches this content. See Google’s privacy documentation for Firebase / Google Cloud.

5.2 RevenueCat, Apple, and Google Play

Used for subscription management and payment processing for TripEase Pro. RevenueCat receives anonymous identifiers and purchase history needed for entitlements. Apple and Google process payments under their own policies.

5.3 OpenStreetMap

If the app displays map tiles from OpenStreetMap, requests go to OpenStreetMap’s tile infrastructure (or a configured tile provider). Those requests typically include your IP address. OpenStreetMap’s usage policies and privacy notices apply to that traffic.

5.4 Website hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit the site, GitHub may process standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) under GitHub’s privacy policy.

5.5 Legal requirements

We may disclose information if required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request, or to protect the rights, safety, and integrity of TripEase and its users.

6. Affiliate and partner links

Partner recommendations in TripEase may include affiliate links to third-party services such as Airalo, Booking.com, Klook, GetYourGuide, Wise, ExpressVPN, Agoda, and similar travel-related providers. If you tap a partner link, you leave the TripEase app and that company’s privacy policy and terms apply. If you complete a qualifying booking or purchase, TripEase may earn a commission at no additional cost to you. Affiliate partners may receive technical information (for example that you arrived from TripEase) according to their own practices.

7. Retention

Support emails are kept only as long as needed to handle your request and for reasonable follow-up or legal records. Purchase entitlement data is retained as needed to provide Pro access, prevent abuse, and meet accounting or tax requirements. On-device preferences remain on your device until you clear app data or uninstall the app.

8. Security

We use industry-standard measures appropriate to a small consumer app (including HTTPS for network traffic and reliance on established providers for content and payments). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; please use a current OS and app version.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Email us at apptripease@gmail.com to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you from support correspondence, subject to legal exceptions.
  • Subscriptions: manage or cancel TripEase Pro in your Apple ID or Google Play subscription settings.
  • Partner links: you can use TripEase without tapping partner recommendations.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK, or certain US states), you may have additional rights such as access, deletion, restriction, or objection. Contact us to exercise them. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

10. Children

TripEase is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact apptripease@gmail.com and we will take appropriate steps.

11. International transfers

Our service providers (including Google, Apple, Google Play / RevenueCat infrastructure, and GitHub) may process data in countries other than your own. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards offered by those providers’ terms and transfer mechanisms.

12. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Material changes will be reflected on this page; continued use of the app or site after an update means you acknowledge the revised policy.
